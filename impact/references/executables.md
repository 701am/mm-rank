# Executables

What MM Rank can ship in one run.

## Always allowed
- Title and meta rewrites
- Heading fixes
- Internal link additions
- Schema JSON-LD drafts
- llms.txt drafts
- Redirect maps
- Editorial outlines and drafts

## Requires human review
- Live CMS changes
- DNS or server config
- Paid link outreach
- Claims not in the truth ledger

## Never
- Invented rankings or traffic
- Guarantees of AI Overview inclusion
- Overwriting live production without a review step
