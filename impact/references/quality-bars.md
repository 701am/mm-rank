# Quality bars

Fail the run's execute step if any bar below is missed. Do not register the action as shipped.

## Every artifact

- Named URL or file
- Named vital
- Verification test that can fail
- No invented metric
- Claims in `truth-ledger.md` or cited to a primary source
- Humanizer on copy that leaves the building

## Titles

- Same job as the H1
- Specific over clever
- 35–65 characters preferred, accuracy wins if it runs long
- Does not clone a sibling URL's title

## Passages meant for citation

- Answer in the first 2–4 sentences under the heading
- One name, number, or date
- Not contradicted on another sampled page

## New URLs

- Deduped against `content-ledger.md`
- Two to five internal links from `link-inventory.md`
- Unique intro. Template chrome is fine. Shared body is not.

## Redirects

- One hop
- Destination is the intent owner, not a dump
- 301/308 for permanent, never 302 a money URL that moved for good
