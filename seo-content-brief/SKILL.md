# Content brief

## Brief template

- Target query + secondaries
- Search intent and winning format
- Reader and job-to-be-done
- Title options (3) and meta
- Outline
- Must-include facts with source placeholders
- E-E-A-T assets required from the client
- Word-count range as a floor, not a goal
- Schema suggestion
- Done-when checklist

Do not draft the full article unless asked. Do not invent statistics.
