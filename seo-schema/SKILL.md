# Schema

## Checks

- Presence of Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList
- Article or Product schema on relevant templates
- FAQ schema where questions are answered
- LocalBusiness on location pages
- Common errors (missing required fields, wrong types)

## Output

- Schema inventory
- Validation notes
- Draft JSON-LD for missing types
