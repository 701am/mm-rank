# Sitemap

## Checks

- Sitemap presence and format
- Coverage of indexable pages
- Status of listed URLs
- Image or news sitemap if relevant

## Output

- Coverage summary
- Issues (orphans, non-indexable URLs listed)
- Draft sitemap if missing or broken
