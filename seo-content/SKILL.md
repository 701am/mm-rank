# Content quality / E-E-A-T

Follow Google Quality Rater concepts. Trust is the dominant idea.

## Checks

- Title vs H1 vs first 100 words alignment
- Meta description that sells the click without cloning the title
- Author byline, bio, and date on informational/YMYL
- Outbound citations to primary sources
- Thin template detection
- Passage citability for AI search

## Output

- E-E-A-T notes with evidence quotes
- Thin / padded / helpful verdict
- Rewrite priorities
